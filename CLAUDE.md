# Sazon Digital — Project Rules

## Global Change Rule
Any change made to the design, layout, content structure, or functionality of a single restaurant analysis page MUST be applied to ALL restaurants in the dataset, including any future restaurants added. Never update just one restaurant in isolation — always treat changes as global templates.

## Data Integrity
All restaurants must be real, verified businesses. No fake/generated restaurants. Every phone number, address, and digital presence data point must be sourced from Google, Yelp, DoorDash, Uber Eats, or the restaurant's own website.

## Geography
All restaurants are currently in Massachusetts (Boston area) and the US East Coast. New restaurants should be in the Eastern time zone unless the user explicitly requests otherwise.

## Target Restaurants
Only add restaurants that DO NOT have their own online ordering system (hasOnlineOrdering: false). These are the ideal sales targets — they are paying commissions to DoorDash/UberEats or have no delivery at all. Restaurants with their own ordering system are not priority targets.

## Services & Pricing
Sazon Digital sells three services bundled in every plan:
1. Website with built-in online ordering system (replaces DoorDash/UberEats commissions)
2. Professional responses to all Google and Yelp reviews
3. 3 AI-generated Reels per week featuring their food

Three pricing plans:
- ARRANQUE: $0 upfront, $497/month, 12-month minimum contract
- INVERSIÓN: $1,497 one-time setup (non-refundable) + $247/month, no minimum
- ANUAL: $3,997/year paid upfront, everything included, no contract
